# Why slaide — sales deck copy & strategy

*A 5-slide, user-facing sales deck. This document is the brief: positioning,
audience, narrative, message hierarchy, and the final slide-by-slide copy.*

---

## 1. Positioning statement

**slaide turns plain text into a beautiful, on-brand presentation — a web deck,
a high-fidelity PDF, and an editable PowerPoint — all from one file.**

The wedge: **AI is brilliant at writing and hopeless at making slides.** Ask any
chatbot to "make me a deck" and you get ugly, generic, broken PowerPoint that you
spend the next two hours fixing. slaide closes that gap. The AI writes the words;
slaide makes them look like a designer made them.

One line: **The AI writes it. slaide makes it gorgeous.**

---

## 2. Target audience

Everyday people who have to make decks and don't have a design team:

- **Founders** pitching investors who need to look credible, fast.
- **Marketers & consultants** turning a doc into a client-ready deck.
- **Sales teams** who rebuild the same deck every week.
- **Students & teachers** who want it to look good without fighting software.

They are *not* technical. They think in outcomes ("make me look good", "stop
wasting my Sunday"), not features. Copy must be plain, warm, and human. No jargon —
no "markdown", "YAML", "export pipeline", "compiler". Talk about the feeling of
a deck that just works.

---

## 3. The core problem → solution narrative

**The enemy:** the blank slide and the ugly AI deck. Everyone now asks AI to write
their content — and it's good at that. But the moment it tries to *build the slides*,
it falls apart: clashing fonts, walls of text, charts that don't render, layouts
that overflow. So you're back to dragging text boxes around at 11pm.

**The turn:** the problem was never the writing. It was the slides. What if the
look were already decided — a real, designed template — and the AI only had to
pour the words in?

**The answer:** that's slaide. You bring the words (or let AI write them). slaide
brings the design — professional layout, your brand colors, real charts, crisp
graphics, smooth transitions. Out comes a deck you can open on the web, hand over
as a flawless PDF, or keep editing in PowerPoint. Same deck, three formats, zero
drift.

**The payoff:** a deck that looks like you hired a designer — in minutes, not a
weekend. Trust the result, for once.

---

## 4. Message hierarchy

1. **Hook (cover):** AI can't make a deck. slaide can.
2. **Problem:** AI writes beautifully, then ruins it the second it builds slides.
3. **Concept (the whole idea, one slide):** You bring the words. slaide brings the
   design — and exports to web, PDF, and PowerPoint.
4. **Proof / features:** charts, smooth transitions, crisp graphics, tables,
   one-click export — shown, not just claimed.
5. **Close / CTA:** Let AI make the deck. For once, love the result. → getslaide.com

Throughline repeated in every footer: *AI writes it. slaide makes it gorgeous.*

## 6. Voice & design notes
- Warm, confident, a little cheeky ("Again."). Short sentences. Verbs.
- Brand: slate→azure gradient on warm cream, ink text; IBM Plex Sans (body),
  Fraunces is available for accents, JetBrains Mono for eyebrows/labels.
- Dark-glow cover & close bookend the warm cream middle for drama.
- Prove the product *with* the product: real chart, real vector art, real slide
  transitions, all rendered by slaide itself.
