These prebuilt UMD bundles are redistributed in this repository (base64-encoded in
`src/render/*-lib.generated.ts`, regenerated by `scripts/dev/vendor-charts.ts`). They
are inlined into a deck's HTML and lazily evaluated in the browser only when the deck
contains the corresponding chart type.
